In-Game Help & Commands

Teams System

  • /t info - View team information
  • /t create [name] - Create a clan
  • /t leave - Leave your team
  • /t kick [player] - Kick a player
  • /t join [name] - Join via invitation
  • /t hq - Teleport to team home
  • /t sethq - Set team home
  • /t invite [player] - Invite a player
  • /t disband - Disband clan (Owner only)

Personal Warps (/go)

  • /go [name] - Teleport to your warp
  • /go set [name] - Set a personal warp
  • /go delete [name] - Delete a warp
  • /go list - List your warps

Teleportation Rules:

For /t hq & /go: Takes ~5 seconds. No player within 30 blocks radius.

Spawn & Protection

/spawn : Takes 15 seconds of teleportation (if not in combat and no movement) and restores spawn protection.

Spawn Protection: Active at spawn. If you leave spawn and re-enter, PvP will be enabled for you inside the spawn area!

Gold Economy & Market

  • /balance - Check your gold balance
  • /deposit [qty] - Deposit gold ingots
  • /buyview - View available sales
  • /sell [id] [qty] [price] - Sell items
  • /buy [id] [qty] - Buy items

You must mine and smelt gold to increase your balance.

Server Rules

Please follow these guidelines to ensure a fair community for everyone.

1. No Cheating or Unfair Modifications

Any client modifications, hacks, X-Ray texture packs, macros, or automated scripts giving an unfair advantage over other players will result in a permanent, unappealable ban from SoupTeams.

2. Respect and Chat Etiquette

Harassment, hate speech, racism, excessive toxicity, advertising other servers, or leaking personal information (doxxing) in public chat is strictly prohibited.

3. Bug Exploitation

If you discover a glitch, duplication method, or map exploit, you must report it immediately to staff on Discord. Using exploits for personal gain or team advantage will lead to rollbacks and bans.

4. Team Regulations

Team betrayals (traitoring) inside claimed territory or allied agreements are managed in-game, but intentional griefing of core team infrastructure via alt accounts is strictly forbidden.
